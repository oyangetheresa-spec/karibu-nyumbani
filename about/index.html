How it started

I started hosting in 2020, in my first year at university. When COVID hit, classes moved online and I began managing my dad's units alongside my studies. At first it was hard, and I wasn't sure I could do it. But the more I hosted, the more lovely guests I met, and they're the reason I'm still doing it.

What I care about most is that nobody feels stranded. I keep my phone available, I listen to complaints, and when something goes wrong we look for the fix together, even for the small things.

I'm also studying software engineering and computer science and learning to code, so I can build better systems for our guests. This website is one of them.

Fun fact: Racecourse Gardens and Talanta Stadium face each other, and from the roof you can see the stadium. It's one of my favourite spots on Ngong Road.

How check-in works

Option 1: Self check-in

Once your payment is in, we send your check-in details, including the lockbox code and the Wi-Fi password. Arrive any time from 12:00 noon and let yourself in.

Option 2: View it first

First time with us and want to see the apartment before paying? Come for a viewing between 8:30 AM and 8:00 PM, when the caretaker is on the compound. After 8:00 PM, check-in details are released once payment is made, for everyone's security.
